          Set up Vimeo publishing

          To let this app put videos on Vimeo, you make a small (free) "app" on Vimeo's own website. It's a one-time job, and it's just how Vimeo lets another program use an account. You won't need to go back to Vimeo's site again afterwards.

          1. Go to developer.vimeo.com/apps and log in with the Vimeo account the sermons go to. (This needs a paid Vimeo plan - Pro, Business or Premium. The free plan won't allow it.)
          2. Click Create App. Give it any name, such as "Sermon Video Editor" - nothing else on that form matters.
          3. On the new app's page, find Redirect URIs (under "Authentication"), add exactly this line, and save: http://localhost:3000/api/vimeo/oauth-callback
          4. Still on that page you'll see a Client Identifier and a Client Secret - two long strings of letters and numbers. Copy each one into the matching box below.
          5. Click Connect to Vimeo below. Vimeo will ask you to log in and allow it; that finishes the job.
          On vimeo.com, open Showcases in the sidebar, click the one you want, then copy the web address at the top of your browser - it looks like vimeo.com/showcase/1234567. Paste the whole address in above.
          This list came from a setting made outside the app when it was installed. Adding or removing a showcase here takes over from it for good.
          The Client Identifier and Secret are set on this machine outside the app and can't be edited here.

          Set up SoundCloud publishing

          To let this app put audio on SoundCloud, you make a small (free) "app" on SoundCloud's own website. It's a one-time job, and it's just how SoundCloud lets another program use an account. This needs an Artist Pro SoundCloud account - lower tiers won't allow it.

          1. Go to developers.soundcloud.com and log in with the SoundCloud account the sermons go to.
          2. Register a new app. Give it any name, such as "Sermon Video Editor" - nothing else on that form matters.
          3. On the app's page, find Redirect URI (sometimes called "Callback URL"), add exactly this line, and save: http://localhost:3000/api/soundcloud/oauth-callback
          4. Still on that page you'll see a Client ID and a Client Secret - two long strings of letters and numbers. Copy each one into the matching box below.
          5. Click Connect to SoundCloud below. SoundCloud will ask you to log in and allow it; that finishes the job.
          On soundcloud.com, open the playlist and copy the web address at the top of your browser - it looks like soundcloud.com/your-account/sets/sermons-2026. Paste the whole address in above. (Only works once connected.)
          This list came from a setting made outside the app when it was installed. Adding or removing a playlist here takes over from it for good.
          The Client ID and Secret are set on this machine outside the app and can't be edited here.

          Here's what will happen when the video is finished:

            Use Claude for title suggestions

            Suggestions made on this computer are free and nothing leaves the building. Claude usually writes better titles, but it needs an Anthropic API key, and the sermon transcript is sent to Anthropic to be read. A suggestion costs a few cents.

            1. Go to console.anthropic.com/settings/keys and sign in (or create an account).
            2. Click Create Key, give it any name, and copy the key it shows you - it starts with sk-ant- and is only shown once.
            3. Paste it below. It's saved on this computer only, and you won't be asked again.

            Set up listening

            To suggest a title, the app first turns the sermon's audio into text. That happens on this computer - the recording isn't sent anywhere, whichever kind of suggestion you pick.

            1. Open the Terminal app and type this, then press return: brew install whisper-cpp (if it says brew isn't found, get it from brew.sh first).
            2. Come back here and press Check again. The app fetches what it needs itself - about 142 MB, once - and you can carry on editing while it does.
