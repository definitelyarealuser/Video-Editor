Connect to Vimeo

Set up Vimeo publishing

To let this app upload directly to Vimeo, create a small (free) "app" on Vimeo's site - a one-time step. This is just how Vimeo lets an outside program connect to an account; you won't need to come back to Vimeo's site again once it's done.

  1. Go to developer.vimeo.com/apps and log in with the Vimeo account you want to publish to. (Requires a Vimeo Pro, Business, or Premium plan - the free plan doesn't allow this kind of connection.)
  2. Click Create App. Give it any name (e.g. "Sermon Video Editor") - the details don't matter, it's just for your own use.
  3. On the new app's page, find Redirect URIs (under "Authentication") and add exactly this, then save: http://localhost:3000/api/vimeo/oauth-callback
  4. Still on that page, copy the Client Identifier and Client Secret into the two boxes below.
  5. Click Connect to Vimeo below and log in with your Vimeo account when it asks - that saves what you entered and finishes the connection in one step.
On vimeo.com, open Showcases in the sidebar and click the one you want, then copy the web address from your browser's address bar - it looks like vimeo.com/showcase/1234567. Paste the whole address in above (or just that number, if you already have it).
This list came from a setting made outside the app when it was installed. Adding or removing a showcase here takes over from it for good.
The Client Identifier and Secret are set on this machine outside the app and can't be edited here.
Connect to SoundCloud

Set up SoundCloud publishing

To let this app upload directly to SoundCloud, create a small (free) "app" on SoundCloud's site - a one-time step. This is just how SoundCloud lets an outside program connect to an account; you won't need to come back to SoundCloud's site again once it's done. Requires an Artist Pro SoundCloud account - lower tiers don't allow this kind of connection.

  1. Go to developers.soundcloud.com and log in with the SoundCloud account you want to publish to.
  2. Register a new app. Give it any name (e.g. "Sermon Video Editor") - the details don't matter, it's just for your own use.
  3. On the app's page, find Redirect URI (or "Callback URL") and add exactly this, then save: http://localhost:3000/api/soundcloud/oauth-callback
  4. Still on that page, copy the Client ID and Client Secret into the two boxes below.
  5. Click Connect to SoundCloud below and log in with your SoundCloud account when it asks - that saves what you entered and finishes the connection in one step.
On soundcloud.com, open your playlist and copy the web address from your browser's address bar - it looks like soundcloud.com/your-account/sets/sermons-2026. Paste the whole address in above; the app looks up the rest. (Only works once connected.)
This list came from a setting made outside the app when it was installed. Adding or removing a playlist here takes over from it for good.
The Client ID and Secret are set on this machine outside the app and can't be edited here.

        Use Claude for title suggestions

        Suggestions from this Mac are free and nothing leaves the building. Claude usually writes better titles, but it needs an Anthropic API key, and the sermon transcript is sent to Anthropic to be read. A suggestion costs a few cents.

        1. Go to console.anthropic.com/settings/keys and sign in (or create an account).
        2. Click Create Key, give it any name, and copy the key it shows you - it starts with sk-ant- and is only shown once.
        3. Paste it below. It's saved on this Mac only, and you won't be asked again.

        Set up transcription

        Title suggestions come from the words of the sermon, so the app needs to turn the audio into text first. That happens on this Mac, using Whisper - the recording isn't uploaded anywhere, whichever suggestion engine you pick.

        1. Open Terminal and run brew install whisper-cpp. (If brew isn't installed, get it from brew.sh first.)
        2. Come back here and press Check again - the app will download the language model it needs by itself the first time it transcribes.
